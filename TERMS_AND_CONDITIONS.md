## 2. Simulation Only — No Real Travel, No Real Booking Payment

**Simworld Air is a flight-booking simulation and entertainment app.** Every flight, route, airport pairing, fare, schedule, seat map, extra, boarding pass, itinerary, and confirmation generated within the App is **entirely synthetic and generated for entertainment purposes only**. It uses real airport and aircraft data to make the simulation feel grounded, but nothing produced by the App is a real, bookable flight, and nothing in the App should be relied upon as real travel information.

- **No real ticket, boarding pass, or reservation is ever issued.** Completing a simulated "booking" does not create a contract of carriage with any airline, does not entitle you to board any real aircraft, and creates only an in-app, cosmetic record for your own entertainment (notifications, achievements, flight log). If any screen visually resembles a real travel document, that's a deliberate design choice for realism only — never present it to any airline, airport, or border authority as if it were genuine.
- **The booking "payment" screen is fake and never charges real money.** The fare/extras/seat/taxi/"total"/payment flow mirrors a real airline booking experience for realism, but it is entirely fictitious: it does not collect or process any real payment, bank, or card details, and no real money is ever charged, authorized, or transferred at any point in the booking flow, regardless of any number or currency-style figure displayed. The only thing ever debited during a simulated booking is your in-app **Miles** balance (Section 5), which itself has no real-world monetary value.
- **No airline affiliation.** Any resemblance between in-app airline names, routes, or aircraft and real-world airlines or aviation bodies is thematic or coincidental and implies no partnership or endorsement.
- **Do not rely on the App for real travel.** Flight times, prices, and airport data generated by the App must never be used for actual travel planning.
